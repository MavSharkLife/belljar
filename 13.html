A New York Times report covered the administration’s move to discontinue school mental-health grants intended to help K–12 schools hire counselors, psychologists, and other mental-health staff.

2022 Congress expands school mental-health support.
2025 Recipients are told awards will not continue after the year.
2026 A later termination plan is blocked for protected grantees in court.

Potential point of failure

When funding stops before a school can replace it, a counselor’s position can stay empty and planned support can end. Later reporting described a rural Kentucky district that could not refill a grant-funded counselor role.

STATUS · 26 SEPTEMBER 2026 A federal court issued a preliminary injunction blocking the new termination plan against protected grantees in a 15-state case. The order does not guarantee payment or resolve grant status nationwide.
